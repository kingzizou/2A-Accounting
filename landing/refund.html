Refund Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

You can ask for a full refund within 7 days of the day we email your licence key, as long as the licence has not been registered. Because every company gets a free trial first, we expect you to test the app before you pay.

  1. Who can ask. The company that paid for the key, once per company.

  2. How to ask. Message us at zizouone96@gmail.com or +233546533134 with your company name, the date you paid and your payment reference. We reply within 2 business days.

  3. How we refund. We send the money by bank transfer or Mobile Money to the account that paid, within 7 business days of approving the request. Bank transfer and Mobile Money charges are deducted.

  4. What happens to the key. The key is deactivated once we refund. Export your records within 14 days of the refund.

  5. If the app does not work as described. If a serious fault stops you using the app and we cannot fix it within 14 days of your report, you get a full refund even after the window above has ended.

  6. Paid twice or the wrong amount. We refund the extra amount in full, whenever you tell us.

  7. After the window. We do not refund a licence period that has started. Nothing renews by itself: when your licence ends, the app stops until you enter a new key. The app shows a reminder in the last 5 days.

  8. Changing plan. To add more users during your licence period, you pay only the difference for the time left. A move to a smaller plan applies when you buy your next licence.
