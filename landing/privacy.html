Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

We collect only what we need to run 2A Prints Accounting, we do not sell it, and you can ask us to show, correct or delete it. This policy follows Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Who is responsible

2A Prints Service, Kasoa New Town, zizouone96@gmail.com. For your account and licence details we are the data controller. For the business records you enter (your customers, suppliers and transactions), you decide what is stored and we only hold it for you.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and sign-in details. Passwords are stored in scrambled form and we cannot read them.
  • Licence: your licence key, company name, user name, email, plan, registration date and expiry date.
  • Your business records: the customers, suppliers, invoices, payments, stock, ledgers and settings you enter.
  • Payment details: we see the payment reference and amount you send us. We do not take card details.
  • Messages: what you send us when you ask for support or a refund.

Why we use it

To create your account and send your licence key, to let the app work and check your licence, to send password reset emails, to give support, and to meet our legal duties such as keeping payment records.

Who else handles it

We use these services to run the app. They process data for us and for no other purpose.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. We share it with others only if the law requires it.

Where it is stored

Our providers’ servers may be outside Ghana. We use providers that protect data to a standard comparable to Ghana’s law.

How long we keep it

Your records stay while your licence is valid and for 90 days after it ends, so you can export them. After that we delete them, or earlier when you ask. We keep payment and licence records for as long as the law requires business records to be kept.

How we protect it

Data travels over secure connections. Each company’s records are separated, and only people you invite can open them. No system is perfectly secure. If you think your login has been misused, tell us straight away. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. Write to zizouone96@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

Children

The app is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will tell you by email or in the app.
