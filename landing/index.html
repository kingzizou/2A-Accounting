What it does

Everything a small business books in a month

Built around how Ghanaian shops, traders and service businesses actually keep their accounts.

Sales and purchase invoices

Raise invoices and orders, record cash or credit, and print them in four designs with your logo.

Payments and withholding tax

Receive and make payments against specific invoices, with withholding tax and VAT withholding handled.

Stock that adds up

FIFO costing, stock take with repeat counts, and quick adjustments for damaged, expired or lost goods.

Expenses and accruals

Enter expenses, pay them later, and see what you owe each supplier at any time.

GRA tax summary

VAT, NHIL, GETFund and COVID-19 levy: output, input and net due for the period, from your own postings.

Fixed assets, journals, bank reconciliation

Asset register, manual journals with vouchers, and reconciliation against your bank statement.

Several companies, several users

Run more than one business under one login, and give each person only the screens they need.

Export, print, share

Every list exports to Excel or CSV and prints or saves as PDF.

    Questions

    Before you buy

    Can I try it first?

    Yes. Download it or open the web app, create your company and use it on the free trial. When you are ready, enter your licence key and carry on with the same data.

    How do I get a licence key?

    Message us your company name, the number of users and the period you want. We confirm the price, you pay by Mobile Money, and once the payment is confirmed your key is sent to your email. In the app, open the licence screen and paste it in. A key belongs to one company and lasts for the period you paid for.

    Is my business data kept separate from other companies?

    Yes. Each company's records are stored separately and only people you invite can open them.

    Does it handle NHIL, GETFund, VAT and withholding tax?

    Yes. Rates are set once in the admin area and applied to sales, purchases and expenses. Withholding tax and VAT withholding are supported on payments, and the tax summary reports VAT, NHIL, GETFund and COVID-19 levy.

    Windows says it protected my PC. Is the app safe?

    Windows shows this notice for any new program that has not built up a download history yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Your files are not touched, and your company data is kept in the cloud under your own login.

    Can I add more people later?

    Yes. Ask us to add users and you pay only the difference for the time left on your licence. Each person gets a role that decides which screens they can use.

    Can I move over from Excel?

    Yes. Add your customers, suppliers, products and opening balances, and carry on from there. Ask us if you want help setting up.
